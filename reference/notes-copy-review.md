# Notes copy review, EN + RU (2026-11-28)

Two independent passes over all eight notes and the index, then merged:
an outside bilingual copy editor, and Claude with the humanizer checklist
(Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing") for English plus a Russian checklist
(calques, officialese, register, agreement). Where both caught the same
line it says **both**. Nothing here is applied yet; Igor picks.

"Igor" = his own post's words. "Draft" = written or translated for the site.

## Verdict

**English** reads competent and clean, but the drafted parts carry a house
cadence a native reader notices: clipped two-beat headings, "X, not Y" pairs
everywhere, cute tautologies ("feel carries the feeling"), and a few
metaphors that don't land ("the wrong pan").

**Russian:** Igor's own lines sound like a person. The drafted translations
read as translated from English: «несёт ощущение», «это не грамматика»,
«правило, а не закон», «работа разная», «Фиг.». Plus one agreement error
and one «ты» slip.

## D. Patterns across notes

1. **Contact ledes are templated** **both**: five open "Not sure…? … a good place to find out" (RU «Не уверены…? На пробном занятии это легко проверить»), and serial/weigh are near twins. Keep the fixed closing; write each note's first sentence fresh, in Igor's voice.
2. **"X, not Y" everywhere**: Real, not current; Series, not cereal; Questions, not orders; Not апи, not сео, not кей; one vowel, never two; in the question, not in please… The most recognisable AI tic on the pages. Keep it in headlines where it's the joke; say the rest plainly.
3. **Repeated images**: "Russian pulls…" four times, "…job" twice. Use each once.
4. **"One of the three mistakes I hear most often"** verbatim in feel and could-you; a reader doesn't know which three → "one of the mistakes I hear most", worded differently in each.
5. **"Tap every sentence"** is wrong on a desktop → "Pick every sentence…".
