# The eight posts as notes: a design and copy review (2026-11-28)

Each post was judged as a future `/notes/<slug>/` page, against what a note
has to do: one mistake a Russian speaker really makes, a figure that shows it
at a glance, three parts (The fix · Why it happens · Practice), and as little
invented material as possible (every invented line becomes a question for
Igor, as NQ3 did).
