# Trifecta Order B — positioning decision (historical record)

**Idea 2 of the 2026-09-13 trifecta brainstorm** proposed retiring the three
thin domain cards for one full dossier section per discipline (Stages
B1–B5, the template, the chess and climbing sections, retiring `#disciplines`,
and a metadata catch-up). Igor dropped that build plan 2026-09-15, along
with its two sibling orders from the same brainstorm
(`TRIFECTA-A-SPECIMENS.md`, `TRIFECTA-C-MARK-NAV.md` — both deleted). None of
B1–B5 is going to be built; the "the largest of the three" framing, the
five-slot template, the stage map, and the "Out of scope" / "Do not" lists
that went with them are gone with the file that had them.

**What survives is Stage B0** — the positioning question this order forced
`CLAUDE.md` to answer, and the material-gap question it raised, which
outlasts this specific order because any future work touching chess or
climbing detail runs into the same gap. Kept here as the record of how the
positioning decision was reached and what's still actually missing.

## Why the positioning question came up

Measured on `main` at 1280px, 2026-09-13: the chess card was 227px / 42
words, the climbing card 227px / 45 words, against roughly 4,000px of
dedicated English argument across the hero, §01 and §02. A discipline that
gets 227px and no mechanism of its own reads as decoration — which prompted
asking Igor whether chess and climbing should stay proof-of-method or become
full secondary offers.

## Stage B0 — the positioning decision and the material

### B0.1 — the collision, stated plainly

`CLAUDE.md` used to record chess and climbing as **proof-of-method, not
services on offer**, with the funnel working as a *credibility anchor*: the
trifecta persuades because the two side disciplines are disinterested
evidence. Igor's 2026-09-12 brief — *explain in detail what my English
coaching offers, chess coaching offers and climbing coaching offers* —
converts proof into product, which is a different, superseding call.

Two targets were on the table:

- **Weighted.** English first and visibly largest; chess and climbing real,
  bookable and secondary. Keeps C6 (§04's mark rests with English lit) and
  J7 (the raised dim floor) intact.
- **Equal.** Three co-equal practices — cleaner symmetry, but it would force
  a hero rewrite, reverse C6's rest state, and rewrite every piece of
  metadata.

> **Decision: Weighted — confirmed 2026-09-13, recorded in `CLAUDE.md`
> under "Content and positioning decisions."** English leads; chess and
> climbing are real, bookable, secondary disciplines, not proof-of-method.
> C6 and J7 stand.

### B0.2 — the material that still does not exist (still open)

Standing Order 8, real material only. There is **no** repo-recorded fact
about chess or climbing *coaching* — 8 years and 100+ clients are English
figures; 2100 and 7c redpoint / 7C Kilter are Igor's own results, not
coaching results. **Nothing may imply a chess or climbing student exists
unless Igor says one does.** This blocks any future feature that wants to
describe a chess or climbing session, not just the retired B1–B3 dossiers:

1. **Who it's for** — one line.
2. **What a session actually is** — the real format: length, online vs. in
   person, which gym or club in Yerevan, what happens in the first ten
   minutes.
3. **What you leave with** — one line.
4. **Whether it is actually sold** — is chess coaching a thing someone can
   book today, or is this a statement of what he could coach? Whatever
   answers this must say the true one.
5. **The RU for all of it** (Standing Order 9).
